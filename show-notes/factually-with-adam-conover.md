# Factually! with Adam Conover: notes for the Spotter

- **The outro Patreon pitch is a host plug. Cut it** (`plug`, `high`), as its own mark.
  It usually starts "I also want to thank everybody who supports the show on Patreon",
  runs through the price tiers, the list of patron names and any joke about a patron's
  message, and ends on the patreon.com/adamconover line and "We'd love to have you as part
  of our wonderful online community." Jokes inside it belong to the pitch.
- **Keep the line about the guest's book** just before it ("If you want to pick up a copy
  of her book... factuallypod.com slash books"). That's Adam sending listeners to the
  guest's work, so it's part of the show. Don't fold it into the Patreon mark, and don't
  mark it.
- **Keep the credits** after the pitch (producers, engineer, "see you next time").
