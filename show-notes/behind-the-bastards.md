# Behind the Bastards: notes for the Spotter

- **Breaks start** one of two ways: Robert leads in ("But first, some ads", sometimes
  with a joke about the episode), or the show goes straight into a host read
  ("Robert Evans here, and I've been telling you...") with no lead-in.
- **Breaks end** with "And we're back." That line is the show. Keep it.
- **"This is an iHeart Podcast. Guaranteed human."** appears at the start and the end.
  Cut it (`promo`, `high`).
- **Robert's host reads are ads**, riffs included. You'll know one by "Robert Evans here"
  plus ad copy: a sponsor name with a promo code, URL or offer (e.g. GhostBed, promo
  code BTB). Inside a break, cut it whole (`sponsor`, `high`).
- **Outside a break, be careful.** The show often talks about companies and products as
  its subject. Mark a read there `high` only if it has real ad copy (a promo code, a URL
  or an offer). Otherwise it's the show, or `medium` at most.
- **Cool Zone Media outro tag. Cut it** (`promo`, `high`): the one sentence "For more from
  Cool Zone Media, visit our website, coolzonemedia.com, or check us out on the iHeartRadio
  app, Apple Podcasts, or wherever you get your podcasts." Keep the line before it ("Behind
  the Bastards is a production of Cool Zone Media"). That's a credit.
- **Repeat sponsors:** GhostBed, Rips on Underdog, BetterHelp, plus iHeart promos for
  other shows. Expect these in every break.
- **Keep:** guests plugging their own shows (e.g. Soren's *Quick Question*), and the
  intro and outro, except for the hosts' own plugs inside them (e.g. "follow us on
  YouTube"). Those get cut like any other host plug.
